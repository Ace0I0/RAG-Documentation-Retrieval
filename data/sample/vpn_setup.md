
# Remote Access Documentation

## Overview

Employees who work remotely must connect to the
company network using the approved VPN client.

The VPN establishes an encrypted connection between
the employee's computer and company resources.

## Installation

Download the approved VPN client from the company's
internal software portal.

Install the application and sign in using your
company credentials.

Employees should contact IT support if the
installation fails.

## Connecting Remotely

Open the VPN client and enter the company VPN
server address provided by the IT department.

Authenticate using your company account and
complete multi-factor authentication when prompted.

Once connected, employees can access authorized
internal resources.

## Troubleshooting

If the VPN connection fails, verify that your
internet connection is working.

Restart the VPN client and attempt to reconnect.

If authentication continues to fail, contact
the IT department for assistance.
